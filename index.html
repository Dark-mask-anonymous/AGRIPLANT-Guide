Planting plan basics

Follow these simple steps to plan a healthy crop.

1. Plan the season

  • Check last frost dates and heat of summer.
  • Match crops to your growing window.

2. Prepare the soil

  • Loosen soil 6-8 inches and add compost.
  • Make sure beds drain well after watering.

3. Plant with spacing

  • Follow depth and spacing recommendations.
  • Label rows to track growth progress.

4. Water and feed

  • Water deeply, less often, to build roots.
  • Mulch to keep moisture and reduce weeds.

5. Monitor and harvest

  • Check weekly for pests and disease signs.
  • Harvest at peak size for best flavor.
